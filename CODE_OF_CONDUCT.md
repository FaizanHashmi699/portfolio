# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participating in this project to be a good experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

This project serves migrants. Contempt for people based on where they come from is
antithetical to the point of the work, and it is not welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be considerate and respectful, especially in disagreement
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming bad
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it kindly
- Focus on what's best for users of the product
- Use welcoming and inclusive language

## What is unacceptable

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion
- Dismissing someone's contribution based on who they are rather than what they wrote

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair corrective
action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate — including editing or removing
contributions, and temporary or permanent bans.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@maqam.ae**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of
whoever reports an incident.
